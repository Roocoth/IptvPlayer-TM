Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how we protect your data.

📅 Effective Date: January 30, 2026

🛡️ Our Commitment

iptv player is committed to protecting your privacy. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly how your data is handled.

  • Your own credentials stay on YOUR device only
  • We do NOT sell your data
  • We do NOT track your viewing habits

📊 Data We Collect

📋 Information We Collect:

  • Subscription Status: We verify your subscription through RevenueCat (our subscription provider)
  • Anonymous Analytics: Crash reports and app performance data (no personal info)
  • Device Info: Basic device model and OS version for compatibility
  • Advertising ID: Used for showing relevant ads (free version only)

🚫 Information We DO NOT Collect:

  • Your IPTV server credentials (stored locally on your device)
  • Your viewing history or content preferences
  • Your payment card or banking details
  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Your location data

💾 Local Data Storage

The following data is stored only on your device and never sent to our servers:

  • IPTV Credentials: Your server URL, username, and password
  • M3U Playlists: Any playlist files you import
  • Favorites: Your saved channels and content
  • Watch History: Your recently watched content
  • App Settings: Your preferences and configurations

🔒 Security: Sensitive data like IPTV credentials are encrypted using industry-standard encryption and stored in secure device storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

📢 Advertising

The free version of iptv player displays advertisements:

  • Ad Provider: Google AdMob
  • Personalization: Ads may be personalized based on your device's advertising settings
  • Pro Users: iptv player Pro subscribers enjoy a completely ad-free experience

🔧 How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads:

iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable for iptv player

Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

🔗 Third-Party Services

iptv player uses the following third-party services:

RevenueCat Google AdMob Apple / Google Payments Firebase

  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status and in-app purchases
  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements (free version only)
  • Apple / Google: Process all subscription payments
  • Firebase: App configuration and crash reporting (anonymized)

⚡ Each service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

📡 Your IPTV Provider

When you use iptv player, you connect directly to your IPTV provider's servers. Please note:

  • Your IPTV provider may collect data about your viewing habits
  • We have no control over your IPTV provider's data practices
  • Please review your IPTV provider's privacy policy

✨ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data stored by the app in Settings
  • Delete: Remove all app data by uninstalling the app
  • Opt-Out: Disable ad tracking in your device settings
  • Clear Data: Clear favorites, history, and credentials in app Settings

👶 Children's Privacy

iptv player is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us immediately.

🔄 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
