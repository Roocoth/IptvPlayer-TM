📖 About Us

iptv player is a lightweight, feature‑rich media player that lets you stream content from your own IPTV subscriptions. We don’t provide or host any channels — we simply give you a beautiful, reliable player that respects your privacy.

  • Supports M3U playlists and Xtream Codes API
  • Free version with optional Pro upgrade (ad‑free)
  • All credentials stay encrypted on your device
  • Built with ❤️ for a smooth viewing experience

Our mission is to make IPTV playback simple, secure, and enjoyable for everyone.

🛟 Support

Need help? Here are quick answers to common questions:

  • How do I add my playlist? Go to Settings → Add Playlist and paste your M3U URL or Xtream details.
  • Playback issues? Check your internet connection and verify that your IPTV provider is online.
  • Subscription management? Subscriptions are handled by Apple / Google. You can cancel anytime from your device settings.
  • Forgot credentials? The app does not store your IPTV password on our servers — you’ll need to retrieve it from your provider.

Still stuck? Our support team is happy to help. Use the contact section below or email us directly.
